## 2. Meta campaign structure

Run **three campaigns** (or three ad sets under one CBO once stable):

### A. Lead generation — Instant Forms (primary volume)

- **Objective:** Leads  
- **Optimization:** Highest volume / quality leads (test both)  
- **Forms:** Pre-fill name, phone, email, city/ZIP; custom questions:
  1. What do you need? (Wood / Vinyl / Carpet / Tile / Not sure)
  2. Approx. sq ft or # of rooms
  3. Timeline (ASAP / 30 days / 60+ days)
- **CTA:** Get free estimate  
- **Follow-up SLA:** Call/text within **5–15 minutes** during business hours

### B. Traffic → landing page (higher intent)

- **Objective:** Traffic or Leads (website)  
- **URL:** Dedicated landing page (this repo) with UTM tags  
- **Pixel events:** `ViewContent`, `Lead`, `Schedule` (or Contact)  
- Use for people who prefer more trust before submitting a form

### C. Retargeting (close the loop)

- Audiences: site visitors 30 days, Instant Form openers who didn’t submit, video viewers 50%+  
- Creative: before/after, financing mention, “still deciding?”  
- Offer: next-day estimate / mobile showroom this week

**Budget split (starter):** Instant Forms 55% · Landing page 25% · Retargeting 20%

## 6. 90-day scale plan

### Days 1–14 — Prove CPL and booking

- Launch 4–6 creatives × 2 offers  
- Cap test spend ~$150–$250/day

- Add lookalike from closed jobs  
- Start retargeting

### Days 45–90 — Hit profit pace

- Raise spend only when booked rate and close rate hold  
- Add Google Local Services Ads + branded Search (see below)  
- Target **16–20 installs/month** with average profit tracked weekly

## 7. Other ideas to support $30k profit

1. **Google Local Services Ads + Google Search**  
   High-intent “hardwood flooring install near me,” “LVP install Hurst,” “carpet installation Keller.” Often cheaper than Meta for closers.

2. **Speed-to-lead as a product**  
   Missed calls and slow replies destroy Meta ROI. Use call-ring-all + SMS auto-reply: “Thanks — this is iStone Floors. Reply with your ZIP and wood/vinyl/carpet/tile.”

3. **Ticket mix strategy**  
   - Vinyl: volume / faster close  
   - Hardwood & tile: higher ticket  
   - Carpet: stairs + bedrooms add-ons  
   Quote **whole-home packages** when two+ rooms are in play.

4. **Referral engine**  
   $150–$300 credit for referred installs. Ask at walkthrough and 7 days post-install.

5. **Realtor / property-manager partnerships**  
   Staging flips and rental turns need vinyl and carpet on repeat. Offer preferred pricing for volume.

6. **Showroom appointment ads**  
   Weekend “design hours” at Precinct Line Rd for undecided high-ticket hardwood shoppers.

7. **Seasonal pushes**  
   Spring move-in, pre-holiday hosting, tax-refund windows, summer waterproof vinyl for pets.

8. **Review flywheel**  
   Text review request Day 2 after install. Use 5-star clips in Meta creative.

9. **Minimum job profitability**  
   Set a floor (e.g. don’t chase sub-$2k jobs on paid ads unless they upsell). Protect margin.

10. **Commercial / multi-unit**  
    Light commercial vinyl and carpet for offices and apartments — fewer clients, larger checks.
